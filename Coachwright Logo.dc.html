04 — Construction & Rationale
THE MARK

One solid form: a barbell collar seen end-on, its mouth cut with two flat, machined faces. The negative space resolves into a hard C — a single glyph, not an outline.

TYPE

Inter Tight, 800 weight, −0.04em tracking on the wordmark. Numbers and labels in JetBrains Mono.

COLOR

Absolute black on white, or reversed. No accent color — the confidence is in the contrast.

CLEARSPACE

Keep clearspace equal to the width of the mark's mouth on all sides. Never crowd it.

MIN SIZE

Mark: 16px. Full lockup: 120px wide. Below that, use the square monogram only.

MOTION

Continuous ring sweep for loading; left-to-right wipe-build + wordmark for launch. Sharp arrival, no overshoot.
