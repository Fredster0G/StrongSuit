**Goal:** Implement automated check-in summaries using the local-first AI models (Qwen3-1.7B) to consolidate client readiness across the roster without breaking the offline doctrine.

- Integrated `generateReply` with a specific `PROMPT` geared towards highlighting amber/red signals across sleep, energy, and readiness bands.
- Ran all tests successfully.
